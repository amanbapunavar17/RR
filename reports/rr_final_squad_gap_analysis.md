## 2. Forensic Gap Identification: The 4 Prioritized Vacancies

While the retained core provides elite top-order stability (Jaiswal, Parag, Jurel) and international bowling firepower (Shami, Archer, Jadeja, Bishnoi), the release of 9 players has created four distinct tactical deficits:

### Priority 1: Reliable Middle-Order Finisher (#5 / #6) — Hetmyer Vacancy
* **The Strategic Problem:** The release of Shimron Hetmyer leaves a massive power vacuum in the death overs (overs 17–20). RR’s remaining lower order comprises Dhruv Jurel, Shubham Dubey, and Donovan Ferreira. While Jurel is an elite clutch runner and situational boundary hitter, RR lacks an established boundary clearer who commands a Pressure Strike Rate $\ge 160.0$ and positive delta lift $\Delta \text{SR} \ge +20.0$.
* **Target Archetype:** Proven death-overs boundary clearer (Indian uncapped preferred to conserve overseas slots, or a high-impact overseas finisher).
* **Primary Released Candidates:**
  1. **Shahrukh Khan** (`3782`) | Indian Uncapped | 168 pressure balls, 163.69 Pressure SR, $\Delta +32.75$ lift.
  2. **Mahipal Lamror** (`4821`) | Indian Uncapped | 55 pressure balls, 185.45 Pressure SR, $\Delta +49.90$ lift.
  3. **Shimron Hetmyer\*** (`3297`) | Overseas Capped | Re-acquisition target if price falls below ₹6.50 Cr.
  4. **Nehal Wadhera** (`7059`) | Indian Uncapped | 197 pressure balls, 136.04 Pressure SR, $\Delta +10.55$ lift.

### Priority 2: Emerging Indian Spin Prospect — Vignesh Puthur Vacancy
* **The Strategic Problem:** With the release of Vignesh Puthur, RR retains three frontline spinners: Ravi Bishnoi (wrist spin), Ravindra Jadeja (finger spin), and Manimaran Siddharth (defensive finger spin). However, RR lacks an uncapped, unheralded domestic spinner with high deception and small-sample mystery who can be nurtured through the Royals Academy for turning tracks (e.g. Sawai Mansingh Stadium / Guwahati).
* **Target Archetype:** Low-cost uncapped domestic spinner (wrist spin or unreadable orthodox) with minimum base price impact (₹30–50 Lakhs).
* **Primary Released Candidates:**
  1. **Atharva Ankolekar** (`7279`) | Indian Uncapped (MI) | Left-arm orthodox spinner, elite control in middle overs.
  2. **Vignesh Puthur** (`48557`) | Indian Uncapped (RR) | 6 middle-overs wickets, unreadable trajectory; re-acquire at ₹30L base price.
  3. **Pravin Dubey** (`2790`) | Indian Uncapped (PBKS) | Deceptive flat trajectory leg-spin.
  4. **AM Ghazanfar\*** (`32862`) | Overseas Capped (AFG) | Mystery finger spin if overseas slot is allocated.

### Priority 3: Indian Pace Depth & Death-Over Enforcer — Kuldeep/Deshpande Vacancy
* **The Strategic Problem:** RR released Kuldeep Sen, Tushar Deshpande, Adam Milne, Kwena Maphaka, and Yudhvir Singh. Although Mohammed Shami, Jofra Archer, Sandeep Sharma, and Nandre Burger form a formidable top 4, Archer and Shami have documented workload and injury histories. An injury to either leaves RR critically thin on Indian pace options, relying on the raw Sushant Mishra and Yash Raj Punja.
* **Target Archetype:** Proven Indian death specialist or 140+ km/h pace enforcer who can defend high-pressure totals at the death.
* **Primary Released Candidates:**
  1. **Akash Madhwal** (`8177`) | Indian Uncapped | 78 pressure balls, 8.69 Pressure Econ, $\Delta -0.91$ compression, premier yorker specialist.
  2. **Kuldeep Sen** (`7545`) | Indian Uncapped | 148 km/h express velocity, $\Delta +0.77$ economy; ideal re-acquisition at ₹75L.
  3. **Shivam Mavi** (`6286`) | Indian Capped | Skilled seam and death bowler at ₹75L base.
  4. **Avesh Khan** (`3653`) / **Deepak Chahar** (`1813`) | Indian Capped | Proven IPL pace assets if price remains rational.

### Priority 4: Tactical Backup All-Rounder
* **The Strategic Problem:** While Ravindra Jadeja and Dasun Shanaka provide primary all-round balance, RR requires a versatile backup (either a hard-hitting lower-order batter who bowls seam/spin, or a flexible utility player) to cover mid-tournament tactical adjustments.
* **Primary Released Candidates:**
  1. **Prashant Veer** (`45966`) | Indian Uncapped | CSK prodigy, 147.83 Pressure SR, clean swing.
  2. **George Linde\*** (`4229`) | Overseas Capped | Left-arm spin (6.51 Pressure Econ) + lower-order power (156.60 SR).
  3. **Zak Foulkes\*** (`29066`) | Overseas Capped | Seam-bowling death all-rounder.
