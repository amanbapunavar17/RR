### Analytical Evaluation of Options:
* **The Dual-Finisher Route (Scenario B1):** Committing ~₹12.50 Cr across two designated finishers consumes 46.2% of RR's remaining purse on batting positions that face an average of only 8–14 deliveries per match. Furthermore, placing both in the XI pushes Ravindra Jadeja down to #8, which underutilizes his elite batting capability.
* **The Specialist Diversification Route (Scenario B2 — RECOMMENDED):** Deploying ₹6.00 Cr for Shahrukh Khan (Uncapped Indian) completely solves the #6 finishing void. This frees RR to deploy an overseas slot on **Spencer Johnson** (145+ km/h left-arm pace angle) and **George Linde** (spin-bowling all-rounder cover), while leaving **₹10.30 Crore** in reserve to comfortably outbid rivals.
