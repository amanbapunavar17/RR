### 3. Mahipal Lomror (`player_id`: `4821`) — The Explosive Spin-Destroying Finisher
* **Why He is a Hidden Gem:** Released by Mumbai Indians at ₹50 Lakhs base. Recorded a staggering **185.45 Pressure Strike Rate across 55 balls ($\Delta +49.90$ lift)**.

* **Tactical Impact on RR:** Perfectly addresses **Priority 2 (Vignesh Puthur departure)**, offering domestic spin depth to support Ravi Bishnoi and Ravindra Jadeja.
* **Auction Strategy:** Uncapped spin set priority. Secure at base price to ₹0.75 Cr.
