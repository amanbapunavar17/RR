## 1. Executive Summary: Turning Clutch Intelligence into Auction Advantage

The Rajasthan Royals analytical strategy for the IPL 2027 Mini-Auction synthesizes **ball-by-ball pressure modeling (Methodology v2)** with **real-world auction economics and contractual realities**.

Rather than naively chasing global superstar names whose prices are inflated by reputation and whose availability is impossible, this strategy identifies **undervalued, high-leverage assets** who statistically elevate their performance when matches are on the line.

### Key Analytical Distinctions:
1. **Pressure Performance Score ($PPS$):** Measures pure on-field execution under high pressure ($P \ge P_{75}$).
2. **Auction Opportunity Score ($AOS$):** Measures availability, squad fit for RR, purse efficiency, and statistical confidence.
3. **Combined Auction Priority Score ($CAPS$):** Unifies cricket quality and strategic viability ($50\% PPS + 50\% AOS$).

## 3. The Five Difference Makers & Vignesh Puthur Spotlight

### 1. Ashutosh Sharma (`8101`) — The Ultimate Indian Finisher
* **Statistical Blueprint:** 217 pressure deliveries faced, 389 runs scored, **179.26 Strike Rate**, $\Delta +33.53$ lift.
* **Why He Changes RR:** RR has struggled to close matches when Hetmyer is dismissed. Ashutosh possesses the highest boundary percentage in the death overs among all domestic batters.
* **Auction Strategy:** If released by Delhi Capitals, bid aggressively up to ₹6.50 Cr.

### 2. Kuldeep Sen (`7545`) — The In-House Express Enforcer
* **Statistical Blueprint:** 7.39 Pressure Economy, $\Delta -1.67$ run-rate compression under pressure, clocked at 148 km/h.
* **Why He Changes RR:** Already on Rajasthan Royals' books. Rather than spending ₹12+ Cr on an overseas quick, retaining and prioritizing Kuldeep provides raw intimidation on Jaipur’s bouncy surface.
* **Action:** Direct squad retention / active playing XI elevation.

### 3. Harpreet Brar (`7325`) — The High-Ceiling Finger Spin Clamp
* **Statistical Blueprint:** 7.44 Pressure Economy, 43.8% dot ball rate under pressure.
* **Why He Changes RR:** Left-arm orthodox spinner who cramps right-handed power hitters in the powerplay and middle overs. Highly economical and contributes handy lower-order runs.
* **Auction Strategy:** Bid up to ₹4.50 Cr in the domestic spin set.

### 4. Nathan Ellis (`7920`) — The Surgical Death Defender
* **Statistical Blueprint:** 8.07 Pressure Economy, 42.1% dot ball rate at the death, unreadable back-of-the-hand slower ball and yorker accuracy.
* **Why He Changes RR:** Perfectly complements Sandeep Sharma. Allows RR to bowl overs 18–20 with unmatched tactical precision.
* **Auction Strategy:** Prime overseas bowling target. Ceiling: ₹5.50 Cr.

### 5. Digvesh Rathi (`58134`) — The Mystery Wrist-Spin Steal
* **Statistical Blueprint:** 8.20 Pressure Economy across 195 high-pressure deliveries. Right-arm leg-spin / googly specialist.
* **Why He Changes RR:** Fly under the radar of major franchise bidding wars. Can be secured at or near base price (₹30–50 Lakhs) while delivering top-tier wicket-taking potential.
* **Auction Strategy:** Uncapped spin set priority. Ceiling: ₹1.20 Cr.

### Special Internal Case Study: Vignesh Puthur (`48557`)
* **Role & Trajectory:** Right-arm unorthodox spinner on Rajasthan Royals' 2026 roster.
* **Data Forensic Breakdown:** 72 legal balls bowled, 109 runs conceded, 6 wickets taken (18.17 Avg, 9.08 Econ).
* **Delivery-Level Context:** Conceded 15 runs in his only extreme-pressure over (Match 40696, over 18). However, all **6 career wickets came in middle overs (overs 7–15)** under medium pressure ($P \in [38.0, 52.0]$).
* **Strategic Verdict:** Vignesh is a **Specialist Middle-Overs Breakthrough Weapon**, not a death bowler. As an internal asset, RR should nurture him in domestic camps for spin-friendly turning pitches rather than viewing him as an auction purchase.
