## 1. Confirmed Retained Core Foundation

Ahead of the IPL 2027 Mini-Auction, Rajasthan Royals possess an exceptionally strong core of 6 retained players that establishes the tactical spine of the playing XI:

1. **Yashasvi Jaiswal** (LH Opening Explosive Batter): Top-tier powerplay boundary striker.
2. **Sanju Samson** (RH Captain / #3 Wicketkeeper-Batter): Elite pace and spin accelerator through overs 1–15.
3. **Riyan Parag** (RH #4 Anchor & Counter-Attacker): Proven run-bank and middle-overs boundary option.
4. **Dhruv Jurel** (RH #5 Float Wicketkeeper-Batter): High-tempo runner, clutch match-situation player.
5. **Shimron Hetmyer** (LH #6 Overseas Finisher): Specialized death-overs boundary hitter.
6. **Sandeep Sharma** (RH Domestic Death Specialist): Premier knuckleball and wide-yorker death defender retained under uncapped rules.

### Tactical Constraints & Rule Realities:
* **Overseas Cap:** Maximum 8 in squad; exactly 4 in the Playing XI (Hetmyer occupies 1 slot).
* **Purse Efficiency:** Premium retentions consume substantial purse. RR cannot engage in speculative bidding wars against cash-rich teams for overseas marquee names.
* **Domestic Premium:** To balance the Playing XI and maintain overseas flexibility for bowling specialists, RR must secure **high-performing Indian domestic talent** in the auction.

## 2. Categorized Tactical Gap Breakdown

### Category 1: Confirmed Core Gaps (Immediate Playing XI Requirements)

#### Gap 1.1: Indian Death Finisher (#6 / #7)
* **The Vulnerability:** RR relies almost exclusively on Shimron Hetmyer to finish high-pressure chases (12+ rpo in overs 17–20). If Hetmyer is dismissed early, Dhruv Jurel is left isolated with bowlers, resulting in stalled chases.
* **Statistical Requirement:** Pressure Strike Rate $\ge 165.0$, Positive Lift $\Delta \text{SR} \ge +25.0$, Boundary Conversion $\ge 20\%$ under $P_{\text{bat}} \ge 55.20$.
* **Primary Targets:**
  * **Ashutosh Sharma** (`8101`): Certified $179.26$ Pressure SR, $\Delta +33.53$ lift, 217 pressure balls. (Conditional target if DC releases).
  * **Shubham Dubey** (`25139`): Internal RR development asset, $155.17$ Pressure SR, domestic finishing record.

#### Gap 1.2: 145+ km/h Indian Express Pace Enforcer
* **The Vulnerability:** While Sandeep Sharma provides elite skill and variation, RR lacks an Indian pacer with express velocity ($145+$ km/h) capable of hitting the hard deck at the Sawai Mansingh Stadium (Jaipur) and intimidating batters in both the powerplay and middle overs.
* **Statistical Requirement:** Delivery speed $145+$ km/h, Pressure dot ball $\% \ge 40\%$, Pressure Economy $\le 8.50$ rpo.
* **Primary Targets:**
  * **Kuldeep Sen** (`7545`): Internal RR asset, $7.39$ Pressure Economy, $\Delta -1.67$ lift, genuine $148$ km/h thunderbolts.
  * **Prasidh Krishna** (`4489`): GT contract ending, $7.72$ Pressure Economy, elite bounce and hard lengths.

* **The Vulnerability:** Absence of an economical left-arm finger spinner who can turn the ball away from right-handers and contribute 15–20 runs off 10 balls at #7.
* **Statistical Requirement:** Pressure Economy $\le 7.50$ rpo, Batting Strike Rate $\ge 135.0$.
* **Primary Targets:**

  * **Mitchell Santner** (`4101`): MI overseas backup, $7.49$ Pressure Economy.

#### Gap 2.2: Tactical Death-Over Yorker Enforcer (Overseas Slot)
* **The Vulnerability:** Defending 10–12 runs in the 19th and 20th overs against power-hitters requires a second specialist partner alongside Sandeep Sharma.
* **Statistical Requirement:** Death Economy $\le 8.50$ rpo under $P_{\text{bowl}} \ge 68.90$, Yorker execution $\% \ge 25\%$.
* **Primary Targets:**
  * **Nathan Ellis** (`7920`): Certified $8.07$ Pressure Economy, world-class dipping slower-balls and toe-crushers.
  * **Richard Ngarava** (`4874`): Left-arm angle, $6.97$ Pressure Economy, tall bounce and death discipline.
