A total of 184 players, including 55 overseas players, have been retained as franchises look to build on their existing combinations and prepare for the upcoming auction.  The franchises have collectively spent ₹1,002.05 crore, leaving a combined purse of ₹297.95 crore for the auction. With 66 squad positions available across the 10 teams, including 25 overseas slots, the franchises will have further opportunities to add to their squads and fine-tune their combinations.  Gujarat Titans (GT) have retained the highest number of players, with 22, while Royal Challengers Bengaluru have the largest overseas contingent, with seven players. Chennai Super Kings, Punjab Kings and Rajasthan Royals have retained 19 players each.   Lucknow Super Giants (18 retentions) will enter the auction with the highest remaining purse of ₹47.45 crore, followed by KKR (₹41.65 crore), Sunrisers Hyderabad (₹40.15 crore) and Delhi Capitals (₹39.50 crore). GT have the smallest remaining purse of ₹8.75 crore, with three slots available.  The TATA IPL Player Auction 2027 will take place on December 16, 2026, at ITC Grand Goa, starting at 11:00 AM IST.
